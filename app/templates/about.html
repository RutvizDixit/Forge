ABOUT FORGE

From scattered information to a defensible decision.

FORGE keeps product information, evidence, uncertainty and traceability visible throughout the decision process.

Evidence first Results stay connected to the information that supports them.
Uncertainty visible Missing information is not silently converted into a failure or a claim.
Explainable matching Requirements are evaluated against available product values rather than names alone.
Traceable decisions Processing events provide a path back toward the originating source.
TEAM FORGE

Developed with engineering discipline.

Team Forge is focused on building practical engineering software that combines structured data, deterministic reasoning, evidence and optional AI assistance without hiding uncertainty.

FORGE is designed as a reviewable decision-support system — not a black-box answer generator.
