IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Missing evidence is not negative evidence.

If a catalogue or document does not provide a specification, FORGE should not silently turn that absence into a claim that the product fails.

Instead, the result can remain uncertain and ask for additional information. This keeps the decision connected to what the sources actually establish.
