WHY TRACE EXISTS

A decision should have a path back to its source.

Traceability makes it possible to inspect how a result was produced instead of treating the final answer as an isolated output.

FORGE records meaningful processing events and links related events together. This gives reviewers a practical path from a decision back toward the information that produced it.
