EVALUATION DIMENSIONS

What the result gets right — and where it falls short.

Requirement coverage —

Were the stated requirements actually addressed?

Evidence coverage —

Does the available evidence support the important conclusions?

Consistency —

Are the claims and values internally consistent?

Uncertainty handling —

Does the result distinguish missing information from actual failure?

HOW TO READ THIS

Confidence should follow evidence.

A polished answer can still be poorly supported. Evaluation therefore looks at the quality of the decision path, not just how convincing the wording is.
